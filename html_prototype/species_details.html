Margalla Hills, Sub-Himalayan foothills, 500–2,200m elevation
description
Basic Description
Fast-growing native pine, well-adapted to dry sub-tropical conditions. Forms dense forests critical for watershed protection and wildlife habitat.
eco
Basic Guidance
Plant in well-drained rocky soils. Best planted at start of monsoon. Space 2–3m apart. No waterlogging. Requires 2–3 years of establishment support.
Advanced ecological recommendations for this species have been intentionally deferred until expert review. Consult a qualified restoration ecologist before large-scale planting.
