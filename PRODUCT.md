# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Community restoration volunteers and coordinators logging seeding, plantation, and monitoring work in outdoor locations with intermittent connectivity.

## Product Purpose

The platform records restoration activities, evidence, locations, and follow-up observations so communities can coordinate credible restoration work.

## Positioning

It distinguishes what was reported from evidence provided, verified activity, and monitored outcomes instead of presenting restoration claims as guaranteed facts.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Mobile-first static HTML prototype. Activities include seeding and plantation; monitoring records capture observations. GPS, media, and offline synchronisation are MVP requirements. No survival estimate is shown as fact without sufficient historical data.

## Evidence on Hand

Product requirements: `community_restoration_platform_mvp.pdf`.

## Product Principles

- Make field logging fast and understandable outdoors.
- State confidence honestly.
- Support community participation without treating it as a paid marketplace.
- Give monitoring the same weight as activity reporting.
