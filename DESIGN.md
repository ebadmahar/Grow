# Design

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Visual World

Field mode: a compact mobile interface with graphite navigation, mineral-white surfaces, forest green utility color, and a single electric-lime signal. Material icons carry brand recognition; no text logo or emoji is used.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans with dense, high-contrast headings and calm, readable supporting copy.

## Components

Soft 16-20px cards, dark rounded bottom dock, 14px form controls, and 160ms transform feedback. The active navigation and primary action use the lime signal.

## Motion

Brief content entrance only, with press feedback. Motion is disabled when reduced motion is requested.
